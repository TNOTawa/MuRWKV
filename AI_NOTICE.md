# AI-Assisted Development Notice

This repository was developed primarily through autonomous and interactive
AI-assisted coding and research workflows. AI agents contributed substantially
to implementation, tests, experiment orchestration, debugging, analysis, and
documentation. The human maintainer defined the research goals, constraints,
experimental decisions, review gates, and release decisions.

Despite extensive parity tests, controlled experiments, and iterative review,
the code and reported results may still contain errors. This repository is
released as a research artifact; independent reproduction and verification are
strongly encouraged.
